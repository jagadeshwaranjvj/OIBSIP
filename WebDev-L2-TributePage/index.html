HIS STORY

About Dr. Kalam

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. Coming from a modest family, he developed a strong interest in learning and science from an early age. His determination and dedication helped him pursue higher education in aerospace engineering.

Kalam began his professional career as a scientist and became closely associated with India's space and defence programmes. He worked with organizations such as DRDO and ISRO and played an important role in the development of India's indigenous missile and space technologies.

His contribution to India's space programme and missile development earned him the popular title "Missile Man of India." He was also involved in important national defence projects and became one of the country's most respected scientific personalities.

In 2002, Kalam became the 11th President of India. During his presidency, he became especially popular among students and young people. He continued to encourage education, innovation and the importance of having ambitious goals for the future of India.
